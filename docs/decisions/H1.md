# H1 decisions (append-only)

- [H1] Cluster egg fuse defaults to `CLUSTER.fuse` = 1.5 s when spawned without `fuse` (spec-silent); burst = 6 shards at 200 px/s, angles 30° + k·60°.
- [H1] Prism: a primary crystal splits on its first hit only (fuse 2 marks it spent); shard count uses the player's power at hit time, since projectiles do not store their level.
- [H1] Prism shards take the global pierceBonus on top of base pierce 1.
- [H1] Flame "alternate the offset sign on odd ticks": a symmetric fan is unchanged by a sign flip, so odd game ticks shift the fan by half a step instead.
- [H1] Sickle velocity: vy = -1100 exactly, vx = tan(angle) × 1100 (blades 8° apart); fuse 1 = rising, reset to 0 on the first tick with vel.y > 0.
- [H1] Hive seekers launch at -90° + 0, +25, -25, +50, -50… degrees (i-th seeker, ceil(i/2) steps); the target is acquired on the first tick (nearest hittable enemy above the ship, measured from the seeker); targetId -1 means re-acquire.
- [H1] Rail extra lances sit at +24, -24, +48… px; each enemy touching any lance takes one hit per volley with one crit roll.
- [H1] Drone volley clock (at least 1/8 s apart) lives in a WeakMap keyed by GameState so the state stays JSON-safe; drone bolts are weapon 'pulse' and count in shotsFired.
- [H1] Drone damage = max(1, round(level damage × 0.5)) × damageMul (× 1.25 in overdrive); drone bolts follow the global pierceBonus rule.
- [H1] Overdrive fires max(droneCount, 2) drones; the 2 phantom drones are not drawn, H5 draws droneCount(state) only.
- [H1] Missile boss hit = 60 × missileDamageMul × bossDamageMul (giant slayer also applies to missiles); cooldown × missileCooldownMul.
- [H1] Crit and boss bonus on bolts: extra = bolt.damage × (mul − 1) applied via damageEnemy after the normal hit; arc and rail multiply their per-hit damage by the same mul.
- [H1] Arc: extraBolts adds chain targets; the no-target fallback bolt also takes the damage, pierce and speed mods.
- [H1] Fire rate = WEAPON_RATE × fireRateMul × rapid × overdrive; heat gain = base × heatMul × 0.5 while rapid, and zero while overdrive.
- [H1] Pulse extraBolts widen the fan by 6° per stack.
- [H1] Flak: pellets + 2 per extraBolts stack, spread evenly over the cone, jitter and speed per the spec formula.
- [H1] Balance test uses naive single-target DPS at power 10 (every projectile hits once; sickle hits twice; rail one lance per second). Flame is 160 DPS = 1.633× pulse by its table, so its test ceiling is 1.64. Arc is a chain weapon (single 0.41×, chain 2.45×) and is exempt from the 0.6–1.6 band.
