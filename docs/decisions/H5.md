# H5 decisions (append-only)
[H5] Enemy shield bubble diameter = radius × 2.4 (spec's "radius × 2.4" read as a diameter multiplier).
[H5] Flame bolt fade uses age / (age + ttl), because ttl counts down; scale 0.6 → 1.6, alpha 1 → 0.3.
[H5] Egg pods crack one frame per 1.2 s of age (clamped to the sprite's frame count).
[H5] Cloaked (invulnerable, non-boss) enemies draw at alpha 0.3 ± 0.08 flicker.
[H5] Environment beams: ch 7 orange flare, ch 10 electric blue, other chapters the boss red (chapter number mod 12).
[H5] Gravity wells: the fx_gravity sprite spans 2 × radius, with 8 swirl dots spiralling inward.
[H5] Lightning strikes every 2.7 s for 0.35 s on the render clock; reduceFlashes scales them by 0.3.
[H5] Trail emitters run only while the sim time advances, so a paused game does not keep emitting.
[H5] Combo popup size = 1 + 0.3 × (mult − 1), capped at 2.2.
[H5] Rail beams: max 8 at once, fade over 0.15 s, impact sparks along the column.
[H5] Attract mode: a boss silhouette crosses every 22 s (9 s pass, alpha 0.12); the chicken parade swaps every 6 s.
[H5] Boss intro splash: 1.5 s, portrait at scale 1.2 in the band 20%–64% of the screen height; keyed by BOSS_INFO name.
[H5] Pool capacity raised from 2000 to 3000 (spec allows up to 3000).
[H5] Classic gift boxes and icons stay in pickups.ts (4 weapons); the six new weapons' art is in loot.ts (registry duplicates removed).
