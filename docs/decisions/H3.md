# H3 decisions (append-only)

- [H3] Shield is an optional `shield` column on `EnemyDef`; `spawnEnemy` scales it like hp (`× enemyHp × chapter hp × enemyHpMul`), bosses get none.
- [H3] Extra behaviors beyond the spec: `jet` (V-formation flight, sine wobble, silent exit past the far side), `mine` (sink with sway, silent at the bottom), `hover` (mothers in a y-band sine). The spec named only `flee` and `blink`.
- [H3] `jets(count, perV)`: `count` is total jets, `perV` per V-formation; V `k` launches at `k × 1.2 s` from alternating sides, lane `k mod lanes`.
- [H3] Every builder's `delay` field is the group start delay (same as `ufo` and `swarm`), so `turrets(4, 3)` starts 3 s after the wave's first group.
- [H3] Ninja cycle: 2.4 s idle, then a 0.6 s cloak, then the knife, so a cloak starts every 3.0 s. Only counted while on screen.
- [H3] Turret burst: three shots at 0, 0.12 and 0.24 s from the burst start; a new burst every 2.5 s.
- [H3] Elite spread: five shots spaced 0.21 rad around the aim, every 3 s.
- [H3] Sniper windup stores its aim angle in `skillState` (8 + angle) because the telegraph hazard can expire before the shot. Shot is 700 px/s × shot speed, along the telegraph line.
- [H3] Mine: detonates silently (no points) within 90 px, and any death or detonation fires 8 shards at fixed 45° spacing (speed 220 × shot speed).
- [H3] Void teleport box x ∈ [120, 1160], y ∈ [80, 360]; only blink voids (the `voids` pattern) teleport. Every void fires a homing orb (speed 160, homing 1.2, ttl 6) every 2.5 s.
- [H3] Mother chicks: seek, speed 200, turn 1.5 rad/s, headings ±0.4 rad around straight down, `counted: false`, cap 24 chicks alive.
- [H3] Pod chicks: speed 240, turn 2.5 rad/s, headings −0.6 / 0 / +0.6 rad around straight down.
- [H3] Healer pulse: heals each other non-boss enemy within 160 px by `ceil(15% of maxHp)`, capped at maxHp; emits one `enemy_heal` per pulse.
- [H3] Bomber cluster eggs and the mech boss egg emit `egg_dropped` (the egg sound); other enemy shots emit `enemy_shot`.
- [H3] Lightning: two telegraph→beam strikes, 0.4 s apart (start to start), each at its own random x. Flares: one telegraph (1.2 s) then a beam (0.6 s), 9 s apart.
- [H3] Environment schedule starts at wave-elapsed 3.0 s and only advances while `wave_active`. The gravity well drifts `x = 640 + 420·sin(0.15·t)` at y = 200 and is refreshed with ttl 0.25 s, so it lapses shortly after the wave ends.
- [H3] Golden chicken: `flee` speed 300 × chapter speed × enemySpeedMul (via the behavior speed scale); zigzag ±80 px at 1.5 Hz.
- [H3] Onslaught boss order duplicates `modes.ts BOSS_RUSH_ORDER` (avoids an import cycle); `tests/h3.onslaught` pins it to the lead's list.
- [H3] Onslaught group cost = enemy HP sum / 4. Each group tries 10 random candidates and takes the first that fits; if none fits, the cheapest is used (over-budget is allowed; at least 90% of waves fit, checked by test).
- [H3] Onslaught boss and mini-boss waves are single-group; sector 1 has no mini-boss at wave 5.
- [H3] Sector names are `Sector n — <name>` (em dash) over a 12-name list that cycles.
- [H3] Chapter 6–12 wave names are the table names; mini-boss waves are `MINI-BOSS`, boss waves `BOSS` (as the v1 chapters do).
- [H3] Sprites: chicken art is drawn in a 60×56 design box scaled to each sprite size (house style of `hens.ts`); `drawEyes`/`drawBeak` are imported from `hens.ts`.
